About

I apply artificial intelligence to photovoltaic materials. My work spans small, carefully curated experimental datasets and simulation and molecular libraries of millions of entries. In every project I check what the models predict against experiment.

Ph.D. thesis (M.S. + Ph.D.): Artificial Intelligence Framework for Optoelectronic Property Prediction in Photovoltaic Materials through Experiment–Computation Integration.

Most of my models are released as public web servers, so other researchers can screen materials without retraining anything.

### About

I develop artificial intelligence frameworks for predicting the optoelectronic properties of photovoltaic materials. My work combines **graph neural networks**, **transfer learning**, and **generative models** with **experimental validation** to accelerate the discovery of perovskite and organic solar cell materials, from datasets of a few hundred devices to millions of molecular structures.

**Thesis:** *Artificial Intelligence Framework for Optoelectronic Property Prediction in Photovoltaic Materials through Experiment–Computation Integration*

---

### Research Areas

| Area | Focus |
|:-----|:------|
| **Graph Deep Learning** | Residual and residual-gated GNNs with transfer learning for multi-property prediction across 2.3M organic molecules |
| **Perovskite Photovoltaics** | Machine-learning identification of dominant recombination losses; 2D Ruddlesden–Popper and Dion–Jacobson perovskites |
| **Organic Semiconductors** | Electronic property prediction and low-band-gap material selection for organic solar cells |
| **Generative Molecular Design** | Property-conditioned SMILES generation (LlaMol) with RDKit validity, novelty, and feasibility screening |
| **Experiment–Computation Integration** | Models benchmarked on standard datasets and validated against experimental measurements |

## About Me

I build **machine learning, deep learning, and generative AI** systems that predict the optoelectronic properties of photovoltaic materials, and I deploy them as **public web tools** for high-throughput materials screening. My work spans perovskite and organic solar cells, from small experimental datasets to millions of molecular structures, always validated against experiment.

- 🔬 **Graph neural networks & transfer learning** for multi-property prediction of organic and photovoltaic materials
- ☀️ **Perovskite solar cells**: recombination-loss analysis, 2D perovskites, compositional screening
- 🧪 **Generative AI** for property-conditioned molecular design (LlaMol, SMILES generation)
- 🌐 **Full-stack research tools**: five public web platforms serving trained models
